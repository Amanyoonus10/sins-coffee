ARABIAN SEA WIND CURATION

The Alchemy of Monsooning

Exposed to damp southwest monsoon winds on open brick yards along the Malabar Coast for 12 weeks, the green beans swell to twice their original size and mature to a golden ivory hue. This centuries-old maritime curing process virtually eliminates sharp acidity, producing a legendary unctuous velvet body and intoxicating notes of toasted spice, caramelized jaggery, and dark cocoa.

THE SACRED CRAFTSMANSHIP

EVERY BEAN HAS A STORY.

From the mist-draped hillsides of Karnataka to the discerning cups of London, each stage is performed with unhurried precision.

01 / SELECTION
STAGE 01 • BABA BUDANGIRI HILLS

Hand-Harvested Shade Cherries

Our master pickers selectively harvest only optimum deep-purple cherries. High altitude shade decelerates ripening, accumulating rich sugars and intricate botanical nuances that define the signature SIN’S COFFEE profile.

  • Altitude: 1,200m – 1,550m above sea level
  • Shade Canopy: Indigenous Rosewood & Jackfruit
  • Sorting: Density floatation & optical hand-culling
02 / ROASTING
STAGE 02 • BRITISH ATELIER ROASTERY

Cast-Iron Drum Micro-Batches

In London, our head roasters monitor bean temperature and development time to the second. By tailoring individual roast curves to each micro-lot, we accentuate natural sweetness, deep cocoa undertones, and toasted spice notes without bitterness.

  • Batch Size: Limited 12kg drums for uniform heat
  • Profile: Slow caramelization of natural fructose
  • Cooling: Rapid ambient air cooling preserves volatile oils
03 / GRINDING
STAGE 03 • MICRON PRECISION

Bimodal Particle Uniformity

Whether destined for an espresso machine, manual pour-over, or French press, each grind profile is calibrated down to the micron using flat titanium burrs to minimize friction heat and prevent micro-fines.

  • Burr Material: Titanium-coated hardened steel
  • Grind Profiles: Whole Bean, Cafetière, Espresso, V60, Chemex
  • Aroma Preservation: Ground fresh upon order dispatch
04 / BREWING
STAGE 04 • EXTRACTION SCIENCE

Balanced Thermal Extraction

Water temperature at precisely 93.5°C interacts with the coffee bed, dissolving vibrant aromatic acids first, followed by decadent cocoa compounds and lingering floral oils, achieving a balanced 19.5% extraction yield.

  • Water Purity: Mineralized soft water (60-80 ppm)
  • Ratio: 1:16 brew formula for harmonious balance
  • Bloom: 45-second gentle degassing release
05 / POURING
STAGE 05 • THE MOMENT OF TRANSCENDENCE

Velvety Crema & Lingering Finish

The climax of the experience. Cascading into fine porcelain, crowned with dense, golden-hued tiger-stripe crema. An intoxicating bouquet fills the room, delivering a luxurious, full-bodied mouthfeel that lingers with distinction.

  • Mouthfeel: Heavy, syrupy, velvet texture
  • Crema: Dense hazelnut and gold marbling
  • Aftertaste: Enduring notes of roasted cardamom and dark chocolate

CHAPTER I

The Seven Sacred Beans of Baba Budan

In 1670, long before coffee became a global commodity, an Indian Sufi mystic named Baba Budan undertook a sacred pilgrimage. While returning from Yemen, he defied a royal decree banning the export of viable coffee beans by strapping seven raw green coffee seeds to his chest.

He planted those seven beans on the cloud-kissed volcanic slopes of the Chandragiri Hills in Chikmagalur, Karnataka. Those original seeds flourished, taking root in deep forest soil and giving birth to the legendary heritage of Indian shade-grown coffee.

“Seven seeds planted in reverence became the bedrock of an empire of flavour.”
CHIKMAGALUR HIGHLANDS • 1,450M
ECOLOGICAL STEWARDSHIP

100% Shade-Grown Beneath Rainforest Trees

Unlike sun-scorched mass plantations, our estates nestle beneath a bi-level canopy of native teak, rosewood, silver oak, and wild jackfruit. This natural shelter preserves bird sanctuaries, mitigates temperature fluctuations, and allows coffee cherries to ripen at half their normal pace.
